## P4 Assigned Feedback
Great work on this website; it was beautifully designed with a professional, cohesive look. I liked your presentation. The chatbot was a neat and creative addition. 

### Overall 
Interesting website. It could be useful for patients to manage their own medical records over time, especially when receiving care in multiple organizations where access may be limited. I am not clear on how audits would work unless the site is interacting with the patient’s medical record. Perhaps theoretically this would be of interest to EMRs 
---

### Organization
The file structure is organized and clear; you could consider organizing all styling .css files together. Good use of components for each function.  The page nesting was organized but required entering many folders to see the code. Consider less nesting and using naming conventions so you can see the bigger picture. 
I liked the color matching of the membership levels. 

---

### README.md
Clear and informative. Your descriptions were easy to understand. I like that you shared the login for a seed patient so people can view the examples.  Consider adding instructions on how to seed a database and removing the MONGO_URI=. 

Overall great project!
